# 📑 Card Catalog Page Design Specification

## Overview
The Card Catalog serves as the comprehensive university reference section of the literary library, featuring mahogany cabinets with brass handles and detailed institutional information.

## Core Concept
- **Institutional Reference**: Complete information about NYC universities and colleges
- **Detailed Profiles**: Comprehensive data about each academic institution
- **Historical Context**: Evolution and significance of each university
- **Interactive Discovery**: Browse and explore institutional offerings

## Layout Structure
- **Catalog Cabinets**: Row of mahogany card catalog cabinets
- **Display Cases**: Illuminated cases showing institutional artifacts
- **Reading Stations**: Individual research desks for detailed study
- **Navigation**: Brass plaque signage and directional elements

## Key Features
- **Institution Profiles**: Detailed information about each university
- **Event Archives**: Historical event data by institution
- **Faculty Spotlights**: Notable academics and their contributions
- **Campus Maps**: Interactive geographical layouts
- **Contact Information**: Updated institutional contact details

## Visual Design
- **Mahogany Cabinets**: Polished wood with brass drawer pulls
- **Brass Hardware**: Engraved labels and ornate handles
- **Leather-bound Cards**: Individual institution information cards
- **Atmospheric Lighting**: Warm reading lamps and display lighting
